# Mike Merrill

**Founder & CEO, KmikeyM**
**The World's First Publicly Traded Person**

## About

K. Mike Merrill is the world's first publicly traded person and founder of KmikeyM. Since 2008, he has operated an unprecedented social experiment that allows shareholders to purchase shares in his life and vote on his personal decisions. What began as a performance piece examining finance has evolved into an ongoing exploration of collective governance structures, personal transparency mechanisms, and experimental economics.

Based in Los Angeles, CA (since 2017), Mike is a veteran of the US Army and describes himself as a "Publicly Traded Person, Former Founder, Veteran, Hamburger Thought Leader."

## Professional Background

Mike's career spans technology, creative industries, and entrepreneurship:

- **Panic** - Ten years at the software company, which he describes as an apprenticeship rather than formal education
- **Chroma** - Co-founder of this protocol software studio (later acquired)
- **Sandwich** - Worked at the ad agency handling both established brands and startups
- **Accelerator Programs** - Participated in Barclay's Fintech Accelerator and Nike+ Accelerator
- **US Army** - Military service as a veteran

His diverse experience includes game design, VR scenario writing, podcast hosting and production, and web-reality shows. Mike completed a fabrication course at an art school but has no college degree, valuing reading and hands-on learning instead.

## Current Focus

Mike currently leads KmikeyM and its portfolio of technology ventures, including Quarterly Systems. He is also the executive producer of **Blippo+**.

His ongoing work with the KmikeyM platform continues to challenge conventional notions of personal autonomy and collective decision-making, demonstrating how "giving up control to others" paradoxically demonstrates personal agency.

## Projects & Ventures

### KmikeyM (2008 - Present)
The world's first personal stock exchange where individuals can buy and trade shares representing ownership stakes in Mike. Shareholders can vote on major life decisions and participate in collective governance. The platform includes:
- Personal stock trading at kmikeym.com
- Shareholder voting mechanisms
- Community engagement through Discord
- Regular updates via Substack newsletter (3,000+ subscribers)

### Quarterly Systems
Technology platform and suite of business applications under the KmikeyM portfolio.

### Blippo+
Executive producer of this current project.
