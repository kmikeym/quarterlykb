Shareholder Poker is the monthly no-limit Texas Hold'em tournament for [[KmikeyM]] shareholders and friends, played online on the second Tuesday of every month at 7pm Pacific, with a $50 buy-in. It has run for over five years, founded and hosted by shareholder Beau Gunderson. Its public home is [poker.kmikeym.com](https://poker.kmikeym.com), which carries the standings, every game's ledger and chip-race chart, the player card sets, and the RSVP form for the next game.

## How it works

Cards are dealt on Poker Now while faces stay on a Zoom call, and both matter: house rules permit the table to openly collude against a player who skips the video. Tournament payouts split the pot 70/30 (or 50/30/20 when it tops $500). Beyond the money there are share bounties for feats like eliminating the current Hope Coin holder, and the Hope Coin itself is a traveling trophy that changes hands only when a challenger lands three kills on its holder.

Each month's game also mints a set of collectible player cards: real players, real stats from the night's hand log, with rarity earned by finish. Winning the night takes the foil.
