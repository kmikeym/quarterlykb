# The MIKE Economy

[[KmikeyM]] publishes a fundamentals layer: six indicators tracking [[Mike Merrill]]'s life and output, updated monthly, with quarterly reports written against them. They live at **[mike.quarterly.systems](https://mike.quarterly.systems)**, which carries the charts, the full history back to 2023, and the machine-readable data.

This page carries the current readings so they can be read here, and so questions about them can be answered without leaving the wiki. **Every number on this page has already been published on the dashboard.** Nothing is first-reported here.

## The short answer

**As of July 2026: healthy and active, financially cushioned but on one-time money rather than income, and steady on everything else.**

That is the honest one-sentence read of the six numbers below. The wealth figure is the one to look at twice: it is high, and it is high because a severance payment from a June layoff landed in the level. Health is genuinely strong. Productivity, reach and reading are all near normal.

If you want the evidence for that sentence, it is the table below, and the charts are at [mike.quarterly.systems](https://mike.quarterly.systems).

## Why a person has economic indicators

A share of KmikeyM has no earnings behind it and no book value to anchor to, so historically the price moved on sentiment (see [[Share Price History]]). The indicators exist to give shareholders something else to reason from: a set of measures, published on a schedule, that can be tracked over time and argued with.

They are modeled loosely on a central bank's data service. Each is an index where **100 is Mike's own baseline**, not a comparison to anyone else. A reading of 111.6 means "better than his normal," not "good compared to other people."

## How to read the notes, not just the numbers

Two of the six readings above are distorted by one-time events, and in both cases the note carries more information than the number. KBER's jump is a file migration, not a person learning faster. PWI's level reflects a severance payment that has already happened and will not repeat.

This is the normal condition rather than an unusual month. A measure of one person's life picks up moves, job changes, injuries and software migrations, and none of those are trends. **The published note is where a distortion gets disclosed**, so a reading without its note is only half the observation.

The indicators also do not aggregate into a single score, deliberately. There is no "MIKE number." Health, wealth, productivity, knowledge, social reach and media consumption move independently, and collapsing them would hide exactly the thing a shareholder is trying to see.

## Cadence

- **Monthly.** All six indicators update early in the month, once every input has landed.
- **Quarterly.** A written report goes out against the quarter's data, in the register format the dashboard uses.
- **Corrections.** A published reading is not revised after the fact. Where a number was later found to be wrong, the record says so alongside it rather than replacing it, so the history stays honest about what was known at the time.
