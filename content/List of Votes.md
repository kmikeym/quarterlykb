# List of Votes

Every binding shareholder vote put to the owners of [[KmikeyM]] since 2008, in the order they were decided. The page is in two parts, because the project has run two different kinds of vote: **governance votes**, which approve or reject, and **follow-through votes**, which choose between options. For what the votes are and how the system works, start at [[Votes]].

Each entry gives the question as it was asked, the month it was decided, and the result. **Every title links to the original vote page.**

> [!info] Reading this list
> **✓ passed. ✗ failed.** The percentage is always the share of votes cast **in favor**, whether the vote won or lost, so a narrow result reads as narrow.
>
> **Related votes are grouped.** Where a decision produced a run of follow-up votes, they sit indented under the parent. Indented votes are ordered by when they opened, because some of them ran at the same time and closing order tells the story backwards.
>
> Dates are when a vote **closed**, which is when the decision was made.

# The governance votes

Binary proposals on kmikeym.com, running from 2008 to 2021. Shareholders approved or rejected them, and the result bound Mike Merrill.

🔒 **Every title in this section links to a page only shareholders can open.** The index of votes is public and the individual pages are not, which is why this list exists: for everyone else it is the only place the questions, the results and the margins can be read.

# The follow-through votes

From the end of 2018 the project ran a second kind of vote on a separate platform, choosing between named options rather than answering yes or no. These decide **which** or **how**, where the votes above decide **whether**.

The two systems ran side by side for nearly three years. In that window the new one already carried more of the load: **41 follow-through votes against 24 binary ones.** The last binary vote closed in October 2021, so the changeover was a handover that had largely already happened rather than a switch that was thrown.

Unlike the votes above, **these pages are fully public.** Anyone can read the proposal, the options, the result and the discussion without an account.

> [!info] Reading this section
> **✓ the decision was carried out. ✗ it was approved and never happened. ○ still open.** Those are Mike's own markers, kept on the voting platform.
>
> The bold text is **the option that won**, with its share of the vote. Where Mike wrote a note about what actually came of it, it appears in his words.

## About the record

**One vote in this list has no original page.** *Facebook Friends Policy*, decided in December 2016, returns a missing-page error on kmikeym.com. It survives here because it was captured from the index before it went. For that vote, this page is the only record.

**The two tracks are recorded differently, and the difference is not tidy.** The binary votes above have a complete record of results and almost no record of what came of them. The follow-through votes below have both, because the voting platform keeps the result and Mike has written a note on most of them saying what actually happened.

**Individual voting is not published.** Both platforms show which shareholders voted, how many shares each held, and in the newer one their comments. None of that is reproduced here, and people named in descriptions are given as a first name and an initial.
