# List of Votes

Every binding shareholder vote held on kmikeym.com between 2008 and 2021, in the order they were decided. This is the complete public record of the **governance track**, the approve-or-reject votes that bound [[Mike Merrill]] to a decision. For what the votes are and how the system works, start at [[Votes]].

Each entry gives the question as it was asked, the month it was decided, the result, and **the percentage that voted yes**. The yes figure is shown for every vote, won or lost, so a narrow result reads as a narrow result.

> [!info] Reading this list
> **🔒 marks a link to the original vote page, which only shareholders can open.** The index of votes is public; the individual pages are not. That is why this list exists: for a non-shareholder it is the only place the questions, the results and the margins can be read.
>
> **Related votes are grouped.** Where a decision produced a run of follow-up votes, they sit indented under the parent. Indented votes are ordered by when they opened, because some of them ran at the same time and closing order tells the story backwards.
>
> Dates are when a vote **closed**, which is when the decision was made.

## About the record

**One vote in this list has no original page.** *Facebook Friends Policy*, decided in December 2016, returns a missing-page error on kmikeym.com. It survives here because it was captured from the index before it went. For that vote, this page is the only record.

**The follow-through votes are not here yet.** From 2018 the project also ran multi-option "which one" votes on a separate platform, deciding *how* rather than *whether*. Those ran alongside the votes on this page rather than replacing them. Their results were never recorded in a consistent form, and they will be added once that record is rebuilt.

**Individual voting is not published.** Original vote pages show which shareholders voted and how many shares each held. That information is deliberately not reproduced here, and people named in the descriptions are given as a first name and an initial.
