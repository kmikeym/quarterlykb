# Joshua Davis

**Joshua Davis** is the journalist who wrote the article that made KmikeyM known outside Portland. His March 2013 feature for *WIRED*, "Meet the Man Who Sold His Fate to Investors at $1 a Share," followed [[Mike Merrill]] for more than a year and remains the single most important piece of press in the project's history. It quadrupled the shareholder base and led directly to a Hollywood option. If you have read one article about KmikeyM, it is probably this one.

## The WIRED feature

Davis reported the story over the course of a year, watching shareholders extend their authority further into Merrill's life while he reported it. The piece ran in *WIRED*'s April 2013 issue and online on March 28, 2013.

A cluster of major coverage landed the same week: *Fast Company*, *CNBC* and *Today*, *The Atlantic*, *Yahoo Finance*, and the *Daily Mail*. The feature quadrupled the shareholder base, taking it past 500. Every subsequent wave of press about KmikeyM has, in one way or another, been downstream of it.

See [[Press and Media Coverage]] for the full record.

## The film option

Fox Searchlight acquired the article in 2014 as a starring and directing vehicle for Jason Bateman under the working title *IPO Man*. The option was not exercised and lapsed in 2016. No film was made.

This is the closest KmikeyM has come to a screen adaptation.

## Who he is

Davis (born 1974) writes about technology, true crime, and unusual people, and is a contributing editor at *WIRED*. His work has also appeared in *The New Yorker*, *The New York Times*, *Men's Journal*, and *Fast Company*.

His books include *The Underdog* (2006), *Entrenched* (2011), *McAfee's Last Stand* (2012), *Spare Parts* (2014), and *The Uncertainty Principle* (2025). *Spare Parts*, about four undocumented teenagers who beat MIT in an underwater robotics competition, was adapted into a 2015 Lionsgate film starring George Lopez, Jamie Lee Curtis, and Marisa Tomei.

In 2013 he co-founded Epic Magazine with Joshuah Bearman, a publication built around long-form nonfiction written to be adapted for film and television. Vox Media acquired it in 2019.

## Connection to KmikeyM

Davis came to the story through the Portland network rather than a pitch. He was introduced in June 2012 by Jona Bechtolt, who later executive produced [[Blippo+]] with Merrill, and he bought shares before he wrote a word. He pitched *WIRED* within three weeks of that first conversation.

That sequence is worth stating plainly, because it is unusual and the article does not hide it: **the reporter was a shareholder in his subject.** Buying in was also, in a project whose whole proposition is that anyone can, the most direct way to observe how it works.
