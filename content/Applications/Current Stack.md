- **Notes**: Combined D3 + K2 for optimal calcium metabolism

**Scientific Benefits**:
- **Vitamin D3 (4,000 IU)**: Essential for calcium absorption and bone density. Meta-analyses show D3 supplementation reduces fracture risk by 15-30%. Also supports immune function (T-cell activation), muscle function, and may reduce risk of autoimmune conditions. Many people are deficient, especially in northern latitudes.
- **Vitamin K2 (100 mcg)**: Activates proteins (osteocalcin, MGP) that direct calcium to bones and teeth rather than soft tissues and arteries. Studies show K2 improves bone mineral density and may reduce arterial calcification by up to 50%. Works synergistically with D3 for optimal calcium metabolism.
- **D3 + K2 Synergy**: D3 increases calcium absorption; K2 ensures it goes to the right places (bones, not arteries). This combination addresses a key concern with high-dose D3 supplementation alone.

**Scientific Benefits**:
- **Strength & Power**: Most researched supplement for performance. Meta-analyses show 5-15% increases in strength and power output. Increases muscle phosphocreatine stores, allowing more ATP regeneration during high-intensity exercise.
- **Muscle Growth**: Studies show 1-2 kg additional lean mass gains when combined with resistance training over 8-12 weeks. Not just water weight - promotes actual muscle protein synthesis.
- **Cognitive Function**: Brain uses significant ATP. Studies show creatine improves working memory, processing speed, and cognitive performance, especially during sleep deprivation or mental fatigue. Particularly beneficial for vegetarians/vegans (who get no dietary creatine).
- **Neuroprotection**: Emerging research suggests potential benefits for brain health, TBI recovery, and neurodegenerative conditions (Parkinson's, Alzheimer's).
- **Safety Profile**: One of the most studied supplements with excellent safety record. No kidney damage in healthy individuals. Loading phase unnecessary - 5g daily is sufficient.
- **Universal Benefit**: ~70% of people are "responders" who see clear benefits. Works for all ages and both sexes.

- **Dosage**: 200 mg elemental magnesium (2 gummies)

- **Brand**: Nature Made High Absorption Magnesium Citrate 200mg gummies
- **Purpose**:
  - Sleep quality improvement
  - Muscle recovery and relaxation
  - Stress and anxiety reduction
  - Supports bone health (synergy with D3/K2)
  - Enhances creatine uptake
- **Started**: [Add date when started]
- **Cost**: [Add cost per bottle]
- **Adherence**: [Track daily consistency]
- **Notes**: Currently experimenting with citrate form (gummies). Citrate has good bioavailability (~30%) but may have mild laxative effect in some people. If GI issues occur, may switch to glycinate form for better tolerability.

**Scientific Benefits**:
- **Deficiency**: 50%+ of Americans are deficient in magnesium. Modern soils and processed foods lack adequate levels.
- **Sleep Quality**: Magnesium regulates GABA receptors and melatonin production. Studies show improvements in sleep quality, sleep time, and early morning awakening. Acts as natural muscle relaxant.
- **Muscle Recovery & Performance**: Required for ATP production and muscle contraction/relaxation. Reduces muscle cramps and soreness. Enhances creatine phosphate stores (synergy with your creatine supplementation).
- **Stress & Anxiety**: Modulates HPA axis and cortisol response. Studies show reduced anxiety symptoms and improved stress resilience. NMDA receptor regulation calms nervous system.
- **Bone Health**: Works synergistically with Vitamin D3 and K2 (your existing stack). Required for proper calcium metabolism and bone mineralization. 60% of body's magnesium is stored in bones.
- **Cardiovascular Health**: Helps regulate blood pressure, supports healthy heart rhythm, reduces inflammation. Complements omega-3 benefits.
- **300+ Enzymatic Reactions**: Cofactor in energy production, protein synthesis, DNA/RNA synthesis, neurotransmitter regulation.
- **Form Matters**: Different forms have varying bioavailability and effects:
  - **Citrate** (current): ~30% bioavailability, good absorption, may have mild laxative effect (can be beneficial for some, problematic for others)
  - **Glycinate**: ~80% bioavailability, minimal laxative effect, glycine itself promotes relaxation (ideal for sleep)
  - **Oxide**: ~4% bioavailability, frequent GI issues (avoid)
  - Currently experimenting with citrate gummies for convenience; may switch to glycinate capsules if better sleep/tolerance benefits observed
