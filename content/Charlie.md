**Charlie** is the investor-relations bot in the [[KmikeyM]] shareholder Discord. Mention him and he answers questions about the market, the votes, and the project's history, using this wiki and the read-only market data on kmikeym.com. He is run by Mike Merrill's operation, but he is not Mike, and he does not speak for Mike.

## What he does

- Answers a direct @-mention in the channels he is allowed in, as a threaded public reply. A mention is the only thing that wakes him.
- Reads the last few messages of the channel before replying, so an answer fits the conversation it lands in.
- Cites facts from this wiki and live numbers from kmikeym.com's market feed: the current price, open offers, recent trades, and the leaderboard. If neither source has the answer, he says he does not have it rather than guessing.
- Reacts with an hourglass when he has heard you and is composing, and with eyes when he has decided a message was not for him.

## What he will not do, on purpose

The limits are part of the design, not gaps waiting to be filled.

- **He does not trade.** He cannot place, change, or cancel an offer, for you or for anyone. If you ask him to, he says so and passes the request to Mike.
- **He does not handle votes.** He can explain a vote and its history; he cannot cast one, count one, or change one. See [[Governance and Legal Structure]] for how votes work.
- **He does not move money or shares.** Withdrawals, transfers, and account questions go to Mike.
- **He gives no price targets and no forward-looking guidance.** He will describe how the market works ([[How the Market Works]]) and what it has done ([[Share Price History]]). He will not tell you where it is going.
- **He never posts unprompted, never sends direct messages, and only speaks in the channels he is allowed in.**
- **He has no memory between messages.** Every reply starts fresh. If a conversation needs him to remember something, say it again.
- **He only knows what is public.** His knowledge is this wiki, the public market feed, and the channel in front of him. Nothing private about Mike or any shareholder reaches him.

## Two things called by similar names

Charlie the Discord bot is not the same thing as the **K5M.bot shareholder account**, which holds shares and posts buy and sell offers on kmikeym.com as a liquidity provider. That account's voting rights were the subject of Vote 179. The Discord bot cannot touch that account's offers any more than yours.

## If he gets something wrong

He can only be as current as this wiki. A number on any page here carries the month it was true; if he states one, he should say "as of" that month. If he states something that is out of date, the fix is a wiki edit, and the shareholder Discord is the place to say so.
