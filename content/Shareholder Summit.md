The Shareholder Summit is [[KmikeyM]]'s annual shareholder meeting: a day-long gathering, in person with a free livestream, where Mike Merrill reports on the year, invited speakers present, and the community that holds shares in him spends a day in the same room. It has run since 2016 and is one of the project's recurring institutions alongside [[Build Week]] and [[Shareholder Poker]]. The summit's own site, [shareholdersummit.com](https://shareholdersummit.com), carries each edition's full program and recap.

## The series

The first edition, in 2016, was called simply "Annual Review," and it ended where these things tend to: with a shareholder asking what happens to their shares when Mike dies. The series picked back up in 2023 and has run annually since. The editions index on the summit site lists 2016, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026.

The Summit is a meeting, not a legislature. Binding decisions happen through [[The New Opportunity Vote|the vote system]] year-round; at the 2026 edition, no votes or governance business were held at all.

## Summit 2026: "Long Confidence"

On Saturday, July 11, 2026, around 35 shareholders and friends spent the day together at the Panic office in downtown Portland, with more watching a free livestream broadcast and directed by Aaron Parecki. It was an in-person event from start to finish: doors opened at 9:30 with coffee and a gift bag, lunch was pizza made to order in the room, and the day ended at a happy hour open to anyone. The theme was Long Confidence: KmikeyM at 18, and what it takes to keep doing your thing long enough that it stops being a "project" and becomes something like a career, but much weirder.

### The program

- **Opening remarks.** Marcus Estes, the day's MC, ran the show and introduced the theme.
- **Keynote: KmikeyM at 18.** Mike Merrill's annual shareholder review, looking back over the year and ahead.
- **Panel: Writing Long Form.** Sheila Heti, Kathryn Borel, and Zach Dundas on the delayed gratification of long-form work. Mike moderated, stepping in when the booked moderator, Chris Higgins, came down with shingles days before. That put Mike on stage for 86 consecutive minutes.
- **Lunch.** Made-to-order pizza by Neven Mrgan.
- **Anonymous Presentation.** An anonymous guest in conversation with Mike about running a business off the books. His pixelated stream feed was routed onto the room's big TV, so even the in-person audience watched him pixelated beside himself.
- **Show & Tell.** Four shareholders, five minutes each, on their own long-running work: Faith Dionne, Chris Higgins (who committed publicly, for the first time, to finishing a documentary he had carried for years), Neven Mrgan, and Jessa Graves, who closed with: "I'm a musician. Not because it's how I make a living, but because it's how I live."
- **Closing Conversation.** Kathryn Borel and Sheila Heti on supporting a creative friend over decades.
- **Guerrilla Happy Hour.** The evening event, co-hosted with Alex Mahan.

### The keynote

The keynote opened with a fake-out. Mike said "the experiment is over" and walked off stage, leaving MC Marcus Estes, who had genuinely not been briefed, to hold the room. Mike returned, delivered the annual shareholder review, and landed the real thesis: the experiment is over, but the project continues. What retired that day was the word. KmikeyM had been called an "experiment" for 18 years; from this Summit forward it is what it demonstrably is, a market in a person, with binding votes and since 2008 a working governance record. The keynote was released on YouTube in November 2026 as ["End of the Experiment"](https://youtu.be/8uCKQS9LVZ4).

### Who made it happen

Venue and support were community-carried: Panic hosted, Aaron Parecki ran the broadcast, Portland Game Library provided totes (printed by Grilka Press, sponsored by PGL co-founder Gene Merrill), and Alex Mahan backed the evening event.

## Watching it

The livestream was free and open. The keynote, "End of the Experiment," was released on the Publicly Traded Person YouTube channel on November 7, 2026, and can be [watched here](https://youtu.be/8uCKQS9LVZ4). As of November 2026 the rest of the program is still being edited; the summit site is where the remaining sessions will appear as they are released. No date for a 2027 edition has been announced.
